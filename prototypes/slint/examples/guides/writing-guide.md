# Writing guide

Rules for anything we publish: docs, READMEs, design notes, incident write-ups.

## Structure

- Lead with the answer. The opening paragraph MUST state the conclusion or the main point before any background.
- End with a summary that tells the reader what to do next.
- Headings MUST be in sentence case.

## Sentences

- Keep sentences under 25 words.
- NEVER use em dashes; start a new sentence instead.
- Do not use parentheticals; if the aside matters, give it a sentence.
- Do not join clauses with semicolons.
- Avoid exclamation marks.

## Register

- Do not hedge. State things plainly unless the claim is genuinely uncertain.
- Cut filler and padding; every sentence should carry new information.
- Do not repeat a point you already made.
- Prefer the active voice.
- Expand uncommon acronyms and define jargon the first time it appears.
- The tone MUST NOT be promotional.
- Do not address the reader as "you".
